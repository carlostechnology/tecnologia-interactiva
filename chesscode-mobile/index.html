Levels & how to play

Read the program, then tap the square where the piece will finish. Tap Check answer. Use Hint for a clue or Trace to see one move at a time. Try as many times as you like.

repeat: run the whole block several times. lightSquare(): test the current square; a1 is dark (blue). onEdge(): rank 1 or 8, or file a or h. evenFile(): b, d, f or h. function: reuse instructions. Moves are limited to the board.

Difficulty colours: green = Beginner (1–6), amber = Intermediate (7–18), red = Advanced (19–30). The bar shows how many levels you have solved; its colour shows the current level’s difficulty.
